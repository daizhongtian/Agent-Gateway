# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Coding Agent Gateway respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, appearance, or viewpoint.

This is an independent community project. Participation here does not represent OpenAI, and project maintainers cannot provide official OpenAI support.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy. Never expose credentials, prompts, project files, logs, personal details, or private reports.
- Give credit and follow applicable licenses.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks;
- trolling, sustained disruption, intimidation, or deliberate humiliation;
- publishing another person's private information or credentials;
- encouraging unauthorized access, destructive testing, or abuse of the Gateway/API;
- impersonating OpenAI, project maintainers, or other contributors;
- any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, security-report interactions, release channels, and public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a non-sensitive moderation concern, use GitHub's built-in report/block tools or open a concise repository issue. Do not repeat abusive content or include private information unnecessarily.

For a confidential concern, use GitHub's private repository reporting/contact features when available. If no private channel is available, open a public issue requesting a private maintainer contact without disclosing identities or incident details. Security vulnerabilities must instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content; close issues or pull requests; issue a warning; temporarily restrict participation; or permanently ban a participant. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, pattern of behavior, and the safety of affected people. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), adapted for this project's available GitHub reporting channels.
